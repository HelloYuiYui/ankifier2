Type the front and back of each card. Nothing here is sent to the AI — the text is used exactly as written. The front is still sent to ElevenLabs for audio.

Tick Cloze? to turn -word:hint- markers in the front into {{ '{{c1::word::hint}}' }}, e.g. Il faut que tu -sois:être- prêt. Everything before the first colon is hidden, everything after it is the hint; leave the colon out (-sois-) for a cloze with no hint. Use several markers for c1, c2, … in order. The markers and hints never reach the audio. A marking hyphen needs a space (or the start/end of the line) outside it and a letter inside it, so est-ce and rendez-vous are left alone. Untick the box and the front is taken completely literally.
